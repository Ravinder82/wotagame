# Product Brief and Roadmap

## Product
WotaGame — Quick Games. Big Fun. A fast, beautifully art-directed, player-first arcade with short browser games on their own webpages. Personal bests and friendly rivalries are the core loop. Social posts are a marketing funnel back to the site, not embedded gameplay inside the X timeline.

## Launch collection
1. **Picture Puzzle** — assemble scattered image fragments into a complete artwork; satisfying drag, snap and reveal feedback.
2. **Hailstorm Run** — a 2D side-scrolling stickman survival runner; move forward and dodge hailstones, chase distance/time.
3. **Crossword** — genuine clue-based crossword, not a word-search grid.
4. **Word Finder** — the existing live game: https://wotagame.vercel.app/g/word-finder-puzzle-3be5f1

Only Word Finder is confirmed playable. Keep the other three Coming Soon until implemented and tested.

## Explicitly out of scope for launch
Rube-Goldberg/domino system puzzles, drone shooting, carrom, racing, general 2D shooting and other brainstorm ideas were not selected for the first four-game launch.

## Visual direction
- Dark ink/navy base, sharp lime accent; use amber/copper and cyan selectively.
- Cinematic lighting, deliberate color grading, bold type, premium textures and clear mobile layout.
- The hero should be an energetic, chaotic-but-composed **game lineup poster** showing game worlds—not primarily a controller, random human, or dog.
- User rejects generic AI-slop, placeholder images, rough SVGs as final hero art, redundant production labels/tags, and Canva dependency.
- Current brand assets: `assets/wotagame-hero.webp` 1800×749, `assets/wotagame-social.jpg` 1200×630, `assets/favicon.png` (built 2026-10-29); earlier approved art archived in `assets/wotagame-hero-assets.zip`.
- Make a distinct, appropriate raster thumbnail for each game; approve one by one. Don't copy the same hero crop into every card.
- Keep accessible contrast, keyboard/focus behavior, usable touch controls and reduced-motion support.

## Features not currently implemented
No user accounts, global leaderboard, cross-device score sync, actual global popularity stats, social auto-posting, or in-timeline game embedding.
